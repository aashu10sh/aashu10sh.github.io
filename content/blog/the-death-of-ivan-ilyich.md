The death of Ivan Ilyich is a grim reminder to all of us about what happens if you do not live life with your own values and principles but let your live be dictated by what is socially correct. When you go off to live a life where every descision you ever make is based on wether society deems it to be a valid descision or not; you are but a slave to the great invisible societial hand.

You do things because its the "accepted" or "logical" thing to do and not because you want to do it. You go on to have a career in the army  not because you love hard work and protecting people but because society says that being an army man is the good, acceptable and appropriate thing to do -  what you want to do in life takes a backsteat and what brings social fame takes precedence - and it is the same with personal relationships too. You marry someone your can stand and like but are not madly in love with because they from a reputable family and it would be a mistake not to marry someone of their stature. The invisible societal hands control you; your friends are all similar social zombies and your life becomes incredebly meaningless and dull.

This is exactly the life that main character of the book Ivan Ilyich leads, He is a upper class burecrat leading a not very meaningful life; everything he has done in his life is because it was the right societal thing to do. He is not particularly in love with his job, nor does he really love his life. He does does what is necessary and works to improve his own social status. This is all well and Ivan Ilyich from a outsider and societal perspective a very successful man, he has a respectable job, has married an appropriate woman and maintains respectable relationships. He lives in autopilot where everything he does is what society deems acceptable.

He is leading a perfectly ordinary life and nothing is wrong with that, what really changes everything for him is when he is forced to come in terms with his own mortality. When his slow but eventual descent into death starts, only then does he realise the grave sin that he has committed. Death is no longer just a mythical event that happens to everyone anymore and it is a real thing creeping just around the corner for him. Only here does he realise that how incredibly meaningless his life has been.

> This touches human nature on how when we are fit and well, we all think that death is an alien concept that will just happen to us one day ( similar to how ivan ilyich tought of this) - only when we face death head on and have to deal with it in our life are we really are forced to come in terms with it. We must try to keep in mind that death is coming and it will be here sooner than we think. memento mori.

When dying Ivan Ilyich realises how truly meaningless all of his relationships have been, his friends are only concerned about who will take Ivan Ilyich's place and his wife cares more about the inconvinience of having a dying husband how it looks from a social perspective, no one really cares about Ivan Ilyich dying and when the topic of him dying is brought up, instead of talking about it and having a honest, real conversation about it they avoid it and chuck it up to "oh you will be fine" this is because accepting Ivan Ilyich's mortality means accepting their own mortality and don't want to do it unless forced to. The only person who accepts Ivan's mortality in the novel is Gerasim, a lowly servant and  He performs simple acts of compassion without worrying about status or appearances. And Ivan slowly begins to understand that this is what genuine human existence looks like. The great and tall Ivan Ilyich finds solace in the shoulders of a lowly servant who lives a meaninful life and accepts Ivan's mortality.
