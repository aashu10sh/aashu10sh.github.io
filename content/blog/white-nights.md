## Aashutosh's Review
This story is a clear example of how unreliable human emotions really are. Human emotions are like colors on a canvas of an abstract painting. There is no structure and meaning (maybe some) to them, they come and they go and in various unexplainable shapes and sizes. Often times the human is unsure of what emotions they are feeling or are supposed to feel, one clear example of this is how: you think you were supposed to feel cathartic joy and bliss after taking revenge on someone who wronged you but you are left with nothing but a deep sense of sadness and unfulfillment.

This is the human condition, cursed with the inability to understand one's own emotional state and given the illusion that one can control said state.

This condition is made cristal clear by both the leads of our story the dreamer and Nastenka. The dreamer is lonely and often dreams of a life with someone, he yearns for love and connection and has idiolised a figure in his mind for which he will fall in love with, he has already in his mind made up the characteristics his lover will have and what she will be like. 

He is, in every sense of the word a **Yearner**, yearning for someone madeup, an ideal entity, that will not match his expectations in the real world. A yearner like the dreamer fails to account for the fact that we are humans and by that very definition we are deeply flawed and no one is perfect. Nastenka is very flawed herself, she waits patiently for a man who may or may not come, for a man who may or may not have cheated her, she tells herself that she loves him deeply but if he had now shown she was willing to love the dreamer and live a life with him. She knows she feels love ( or does she ) but she does not know who she feels this love for.Just like the dreamer, her lover has become a almost fictitious idol and a perfect entity in her mind. 

Both our characters have dwelled too long in the land of the dreams and allowed mistaking a dreamt perfect idiolized version with the real deal and this has been their biggest sin. Its important to dream but its also important that we come down to grasslands once in a while.
